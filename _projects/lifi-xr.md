Wireless communication dominates the energy budget of augmented-reality devices, and radio links offer no natural notion of *where* the user is. Visible light communication (Li-Fi) addresses both: optical receivers can be lower power than radios, and light's line-of-sight propagation spatially confines the data.

**Li-FiAR** (S3 workshop, MobiCom '24) integrates a Li-Fi transmitter and receiver with an AR device and demonstrates streaming images and audio to the headset over light.

**VisibleBits** (MobiCom '25 demo) builds on this to create *information spotlights*: localized optical zones that deliver context-specific content, such as museum exhibit details, to a Meta Quest 3 as the user walks into the illuminated area, without radio-based localization.
